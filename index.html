Everything You Need to Know

Answers to the most searched questions about crypto debit cards — KYC, fees, limits, and more.

A crypto debit card allows you to spend your cryptocurrency at any merchant that accepts traditional debit cards (like Visa or Mastercard). When you make a purchase, the card provider automatically converts your crypto into the local fiat currency (like USD or EUR) in real-time to complete the transaction. Most cards, like RedotPay or Bybit Card, offer both virtual cards for online shopping and physical cards for in-person use.

Yes, but not always. There are No-KYC or Low-KYC options that only require an email or phone number for basic tiers with lower spending limits (e.g., $500 – $1,000). However, to access higher limits, ATM withdrawals, or a physical card, most providers will eventually require standard ID verification due to global financial regulations.

Most modern crypto cards (RedotPay, Bybit Card) have $0 monthly maintenance fees. Common fees include a one-time issuance fee for the card ($10–$100), a small conversion fee (usually around 1%) when swapping crypto to fiat, and ATM withdrawal fees (typically 2% or a flat fee). Always check the specific fee schedule in the app before ordering.
